The Berlin Ring Shortlist

Every model here is real, orderable, and sold from inside the EU. Prices come from each seller’s own page, read on 17 August 2026.

Rule 1 — buy inside the EU

A ring from the US or UK adds 2.5% duty, 19% import VAT and a clearance fee: about +23%. Duties are not refunded if you return it.

Rule 2 — IGI for lab stones

GIA stopped applying the 4Cs to lab-grown diamonds in October 2025. Only IGI still gives you a real colour and clarity grade.

Rule 3 — order EU 51, engrave later

Sizing down is cheap; sizing up means adding metal. An engraving voids your 14-day right to return under §312g BGB.
